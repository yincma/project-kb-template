# Review Notes

Review generated Obsidian notes.

Checklist:
- YAML frontmatter exists.
- `status` is one of `draft`, `needs_review`, `reviewed`, `deprecated`.
- AI-generated notes default to `needs_review`.
- `source_refs` are structured.
- Factual claims have evidence.
- Unsupported content is under `Assumptions` or `Evidence Gaps`.
- Obsidian links are useful.
- Notes are small and readable.
- Notes are suitable for Project KB indexing.
- `status: reviewed` files were not overwritten.
